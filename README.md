# Creature from the Krusty Krab Decompilation
Decompilation of the Wii version of Creature from the Krusty Krab. Also known as Nighty Nightmare. [Breature from the Krusty Karb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tHg_WM6eEM&t=40s). [SB06 - 40 Winks](https://youtu.be/sgZh8MLzfyg?t=1313). SpongeBob Revolution. The strangest game you've ever played.

Assets from the original game are not included. Get your own assets! (preferably from a legal copy of the game, but i'm not your mother, so whatever)

> [!NOTE]
> We would prefer if you didn't use Claude to contribute. Or Codex. Or Copilot. Or Karen. That includes you, Plankton.

You should preferably use the [NTSC-U/USA version](https://redump.info/disc/8504). The [PAL version](https://redump.info/disc/37743) has not been tested.
