# Creature from the Krusty Krab Decompilation
Decompilation of the GameCube version of Creature from the Krusty Krab. Also known as Nighty Nightmare. [Breature from the Krusty Karb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tHg_WM6eEM&t=40s). [SB06 - 40 Winks](https://youtu.be/sgZh8MLzfyg?t=1313). The strangest game you've ever played.

Assets from the original game are not included. Get your own assets! (preferably from a legal copy of the game, but i'm not your mother, so whatever)

> [!NOTE]
> We would prefer if you didn't use Claude to contribute. Or Codex. Or Copilot. Or Karen. That includes you, Plankton.

You should preferably use the [NTSC-U/USA version](https://redump.info/disc/9028). The [PAL version](https://redump.info/disc/22834) has not been tested, although it is known to also contain debug symbols. Who knows? Maybe you could even port [these fixes](https://tcrf.net/SpongeBob_SquarePants:_Creature_from_the_Krusty_Krab_(PlayStation_2,_GameCube,_Wii)/Platform_Differences#:~:text=Notably%2C%20the%20European%20GameCube%20release%20actually%20fixed%20both%20of%20these%20issues) to the NTSC-U/USA version!
